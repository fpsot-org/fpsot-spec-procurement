= Neutrality
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

A body that writes specifications and clause text sits close to each tender that cites them.
The {abbr-fpsot-first} takes no contracts, so it gains nothing from any tender.
These three rules make that position public and keep it true.

[[PRC-050]]
*PRC-050.* {abbr-fpsot} MUST NOT bid on any tender, alone or with others.
It MUST NOT act as a subcontractor under any tender, and MUST NOT take paid work from one.
It MUST state this rule in public, on its website and in each {term-clause-pack}.
Québec bodies treat a supplier that helps prepare a tender as a possible conflict of interest.
The link:https://cdn-contenu.quebec.ca/cdn-contenu/curateur-public/pdf/polit_direct_proc/organisat/ORG-120.pdf[contract policy of the Curateur public] is an example.
A body that never bids cannot draft a clause to win one.

[[PRC-051]]
*PRC-051.* Each person who contributes to a pack or a specification MUST declare their commercial interests in a public register.
It MUST name each employer or client that sells the products or services of the contribution to public bodies.
The contributor MUST update it within 30 days of a change.
The {abbr-cfta-first} speaks to this risk in article 509 of its link:https://www.cfta-alec.ca/cfta-agreement/chapter-five-government-procurement[chapter on procurement].
It bars a buyer from advice that limits competition, from a person with a commercial interest.
A public declaration lets a buyer check where the text came from.

[[PRC-052]]
*PRC-052.* {abbr-fpsot} MUST give help with drafting a tender only through public channels.
These channels are a published list of questions and answers, and office hours open to anyone.
The foundation MUST add each answer from the office hours to the published list.
It MUST NOT name the buyer unless the buyer agrees.
The foundation MUST NOT give private help to one buyer on one tender.
Help that every bidder can see gives no bidder an edge.
