= FPSOT procurement specification
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

This specification sets how the {abbr-fpsot-first} writes and maintains the text that public bodies paste into their tenders.
That text comes as {term-clause-pack}s of {term-clause-module}s.
The specification also sets when an {abbr-fpsot} specification is ready for a tender to cite it.

A public body that buys software should require what a product does, not who made it.
Each module therefore requires conformance to a versioned {abbr-fpsot} specification, proven by an open test suite or by equivalent evidence.
No module ever requires software that {abbr-fpsot} makes.

The rules come in six parts, and two informative pages follow them.

* xref:packs.adoc[Clause packs] sets what a pack is, its versions, its languages and its license.
* xref:modules.adoc[Clause modules] sets the form of each module, its variants by legal regime and its sources.
* xref:drafting.adoc[The drafting rule] sets how a module cites a specification and what evidence it asks for.
* xref:content.adoc[Content of the modules] sets what each of the nine modules covers, including award and cost.
* xref:readiness.adoc[Readiness and results] sets when a pack for one specification may leave draft status, and the public register of results.
* xref:neutrality.adoc[Neutrality] sets why and how {abbr-fpsot} stays out of every tender.
* xref:routes.adoc[Routes to market] is informative, and describes the ways to buy that suit open specifications.
* xref:counsel.adoc[Questions for counsel] is informative, and lists the points that a lawyer must settle first.

== The clause modules

The draft modules live in the folder `clauses/` of this {term-repository}, in English in `clauses/en/` and in French in `clauses/fr/`.
They are not part of this book.
Each module is a draft, and says so at its top.
A buyer must not use a module in a tender before a lawyer reviews it.

The modules are plain-text Markdown files.
The continuous integration builds each one as an OpenDocument file and a Word file, under PRC-008.

== Two licenses

This specification is under the Community Specification License 1.0, like every {abbr-fpsot} specification.
The clause modules are under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license.
Its full legal code is in `clauses/LICENSE`.
A buyer may copy, change and publish a module in a tender, provided it credits the source under PRC-006.

== Related specifications

This specification builds on other {abbr-fpsot} specifications.
The testing specification sets the conformance suites that a module asks for, in TST-044 to TST-048.
The accessibility specification sets the requirements of a call for tenders, in ACC-055 to ACC-058.
The security specification sets the bill of materials, the private reporting channel and the `SECURITY.md` file.
The internationalization specification sets the languages of a product, and the data models specification sets the form of exported data.
The documentation specification sets the editable formats, in DOC-042, and the copyright notice of each output, in DOC-047.

The future {abbr-fpsot} specifications on code and on capabilities will define building blocks that a tender may also cite.
This version does not depend on them.

== Conformance

[quote, 'link:https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc8174[RFC 8174]']
____
The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", "REQUIRED", "SHALL", "SHALL NOT", "SHOULD", "SHOULD NOT", "RECOMMENDED", "NOT RECOMMENDED", "MAY", and "OPTIONAL" in this document are to be interpreted as described in BCP 14 (link:https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc2119[RFC 2119], link:https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc8174[RFC 8174]) when, and only when, they appear in all capitals, as shown here.
____

The keywords follow requirement WRT-010 of the {abbr-fpsot} writing specification.
The French version uses the French keywords of WRT-011.

There are two conformance classes.

* A *clause pack* conforms when it and its modules meet every MUST rule on packs, modules, drafting, content and readiness.
* *{abbr-fpsot}* conforms as a publisher when it meets every MUST rule on readiness, results and neutrality.

A tender that a public body writes is not a conforming product of this specification.
The body may use a module as it sees fit, under its own law.

== Legal status

This specification states the practice of {abbr-fpsot}, and it is not legal advice.
The modules are drafts, and are not legal advice either.
Points that need a lawyer sit in informative notes and on the page xref:counsel.adoc[Questions for counsel], never in a rule.
