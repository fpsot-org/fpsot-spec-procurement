## Drafting note

Remove this note before you publish the tender.

**Purpose.** This clause sets what the bidder gives to show conformance, when, and who may produce it.
It also keeps the supplier conforming after the award.

**Functional need.** A promise of conformance is not proof.
A machine-readable test report lets you compare bids requirement by requirement, and lets you run the same tests at acceptance.

**Your choices.**

- Decide whether you test the highest-ranked bid before the award.
- Set the time the supplier has to fix a gap in an optional class, or a regression after acceptance. A failed requirement of a required class rejects the bid.
- Keep the words "or equivalent" wherever the clause names the suite.

**Structure.** The cycle of evidence at the award, plan for each gap and yearly renewal follows the federal accessibility procurement pattern of the GC Catalogue.
No text of that pattern is adapted.

**Mandatory reference.** Only the normative FPSOT specification is the mandatory reference.
Cite that specification as the requirement, never this module or an FPSOT guide.
A suite result is not a certification, an approval or an endorsement by FPSOT. Do not describe it so.

**Not legal advice.** This module is a draft and is not legal advice.

## Clause text

### Text for all regimes

This text is the same for Québec public bodies (LCOP and RCTI), Québec municipal bodies (LCOM) and the federal government.
Replace "the buyer" with the name your documents use, such as "the organization", "the municipal body" or "Canada".

1. **Conformance statement.** With its bid, the bidder must give a conformance statement. For each conformance class that module M01 requires, the statement says "yes" or "no". The buyer rejects a bid whose statement says "no" for a required class.
2. **Test report.** With its bid, the bidder must also give the machine-readable report of the conformance suite of the specification, release [suite release], or equivalent. The report must name the release of the suite and give the result of each requirement. In place of this report, the bidder may give an equivalent test report. The statement alone is not proof.
3. **Rejection.** The buyer rejects a bid whose report shows a failed requirement in a required class.
4. **Who runs the tests.** The bidder, a third party that the bidder chooses, or the buyer may run the suite, or equivalent tests. The same parties may produce an equivalent test report. FPSOT does not run, check or approve any test.
5. **Test before award.** [Option: Before the award, the buyer may run the suite, or equivalent tests, against the solution of the highest-ranked bidder. A failed test of a required class leads to the rejection of that bid.]
6. **Acceptance.** Before final acceptance, the buyer may run the same suite, or equivalent tests, against the delivered solution.
7. **Gaps.** For each failed requirement of an optional class that the bid offered, the supplier must give a plan to fix it. The supplier must do the same when a required class regresses after acceptance. Each plan gives a date for each fix, no later than [time to fix] after the report that shows the gap.
8. **Renewal.** The supplier must give a new statement and a new report with each major or minor release of the solution. It must also give them at least once a year. When nothing changed, the supplier may confirm this in writing instead.
9. **No endorsement.** A conformance result is not a certification, an approval or an endorsement by FPSOT.

## Fields

| Field | Meaning | Suggested value |
|---|---|---|
| [suite release] | The release of the conformance suite that tests the version of module M01. Its major and minor numbers match that version. | The latest release for that version |
| [time to fix] | The longest time the supplier has to fix a gap in an optional class, or a regression. | 90 days |
