## Drafting note

Remove this note before you publish the tender.

**Purpose.** This clause requires an accessible solution, proof of it in the bid and at the award, and fixes within an agreed time.
It applies rules ACC-055 to ACC-058 of the FPSOT accessibility specification, and changes none of its values.

**Functional need.** Public services must work for everyone, including people who use assistive technologies.

**Your choices.**

- Set the time the supplier has to fix a failure.
- Give accessibility a weight in the scoring of module M08.

**Regimes.** Québec bodies add the Québec standard SGQRI 008 3.0. Federal bodies add the Canadian edition of EN 301 549.

**Mandatory reference.** Only the normative texts that this clause names are the mandatory references.
Cite those texts as the requirement, never this module or an FPSOT guide.

**Not legal advice.** This module is a draft and is not legal advice.

## Clause text

### Variant for Québec public bodies (LCOP and RCTI)

1. The solution must meet the FPSOT accessibility specification, version [x.y], rules ACC-001 to ACC-003, or equivalent. These rules require WCAG 2.2 at level AA and four further criteria.
2. The solution must also meet EN 301 549 V4.1.1, and the Québec standard SGQRI 008 3.0, or equivalent.
3. With its bid, the bidder must give a conformance report with a gap analysis against each required standard. The report must rest on a test record that meets rule ACC-045 of the FPSOT accessibility specification, or equivalent.
4. At the award, the supplier must give an updated conformance report.
5. The supplier must fix each accessibility failure within [time to fix] of its report.
6. The supplier must give a new conformance report with each major release.

### Variant for Québec municipal bodies (LCOM)

1. The solution must meet the FPSOT accessibility specification, version [x.y], rules ACC-001 to ACC-003, or equivalent. These rules require WCAG 2.2 at level AA and four further criteria.
2. The solution must also meet EN 301 549 V4.1.1, and the Québec standard SGQRI 008 3.0, or equivalent.
3. With its bid, the bidder must give a conformance report with a gap analysis against each required standard. The report must rest on a test record that meets rule ACC-045 of the FPSOT accessibility specification, or equivalent.
4. At the award, the supplier must give an updated conformance report.
5. The supplier must fix each accessibility failure within [time to fix] of its report.
6. The supplier must give a new conformance report with each major release.

### Variant for the federal government

1. The Work must meet the FPSOT accessibility specification, version [x.y], rules ACC-001 to ACC-003, or equivalent. These rules require WCAG 2.2 at level AA and four further criteria.
2. The Work must also meet EN 301 549 V4.1.1 and CAN/ASC - EN 301 549:2024, or equivalent.
3. With its bid, the Bidder must give a conformance report with a gap analysis against each required standard. The report must rest on a test record that meets rule ACC-045 of the FPSOT accessibility specification, or equivalent.
4. At contract award, the Contractor must give an updated conformance report.
5. The Contractor must fix each accessibility failure within [time to fix] of its report.
6. The Contractor must give a new conformance report with each major release.

## Fields

| Field | Meaning | Suggested value |
|---|---|---|
| [x.y] | The version of the FPSOT accessibility specification. | 0.1 |
| [time to fix] | The longest time to fix a failure. | 30 days for a blocking failure, 90 days for others |
