## What this pack is

This pack holds nine clause modules that a public body can adapt and paste into its own tender documents.
Each module fits into the official tender documents of the buyer, as an annex or a section.
It does not replace the general administrative clauses of the buyer.

This pack is not normative.
Only the normative FPSOT specification that a tender cites is the mandatory reference.
A tender must cite that specification, with its version and conformance classes.
It must not cite this pack, a module or an FPSOT guide as a required technology.

This pack is not legal advice.
Each buyer must have its own counsel review each clause before it uses it.
The page "Questions for counsel" of the FPSOT procurement specification lists the open legal points.

## Language

For use in Québec, the French text of this pack and of each cited specification is the reference version.
For use elsewhere, the English and French texts have the same standing.

## Neutrality

FPSOT does not bid on any tender, alone or with others.
It does not act as a subcontractor under any tender, and takes no paid work from one.
FPSOT gives help with drafting a tender only through its published questions and answers and its open office hours.
It gives no private help to one buyer on one tender, and it judges no bid.

## Thresholds of the trade agreements

Thresholds change every two years.
Check the values in force on the date of your tender with the source below.

Source: Secrétariat du Conseil du trésor du Québec, *Accords de libéralisation des marchés publics, seuils d'application*, valid from 2026-01-01.
FPSOT last checked this table on 2026-11-08.
The table below repeats the values for contracts of services and of supply, in Canadian dollars.

| Kind of body (Québec) | Contract | CFTA | Québec–Ontario | CETA | WTO GPA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ministries and budget-funded bodies | Services | 139,000 | 139,000 | 368,000 | 653,200 |
| Ministries and budget-funded bodies | Supply | 34,700 | 34,700 | 368,000 | 653,200 |
| Other government bodies | Services | 139,000 | 139,000 | 368,000 | not listed |
| Other government bodies | Supply | 34,700 | 34,700 | 368,000 | not listed |
| Education, and health and social services | Services | 139,000 | 139,000 | 368,000 | not listed |
| Education, and health and social services | Supply | 139,000 | 139,000 | 368,000 | not listed |

Municipal bodies under the LCOM must use an open procedure from 139,000 dollars since 2026-01-01, according to the guide of the Québec ministry of municipal affairs.
Federal buyers must check the thresholds that apply to their own contracts.
This pack gives no federal values.

## Sources

The modules are original FPSOT text.
They adapt no text from another source.
These sources inspired their structure, and are cited only:

- World Bank, *Technical Procurement Guidance for DPI and Integral Digital Services*, 2026. Its license allows only noncommercial reuse, so no text is adapted.
- Government of Canada, GC Catalogue, *Accessibility Procurement Language*. Its cycle of conformance reports inspired module M02.
- Province of British Columbia, *Code With Us* terms. Their CC BY-SA 4.0 license would spread to derived text, so no text is adapted.
- European Commission, Interoperable Europe, guidelines on public procurement. Their pass or fail baseline and 0 to 5 scales inspired module M08.
