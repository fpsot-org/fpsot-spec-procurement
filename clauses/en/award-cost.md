## Drafting note

Remove this note before you publish the tender.

**Purpose.** This clause sets how the buyer evaluates bids: pass or fail for what every bid must meet, points only for what goes beyond it, and a price that counts the costs the buyer will bear over the life of the solution.

**Functional need.** Lock-in shows up as license, change and migration costs.
A worksheet that quantifies them before the tender makes them visible at the award.

**Your choices.**

- Keep or change the default weights. They must total 100.
- Decide whether you add the optional criterion on accessibility beyond the baseline, and take its weight from the others.
- Fill in the cost-element worksheet before you publish the tender. Each element must be quantifiable and measurable, and the tender documents must name it.
- Choose the method of award that your regime allows. Municipal bodies choose one of the two LCOM sub-variants.

**Québec public bodies (RCTI).** The text below follows the consolidated text of the RCTI current to 2026-10-02. Each article and section number is to be verified.

- Annex 2 asks for at least 3 criteria, each scored from 0 to 100 points. For each criterion, the tender documents must state the elements of quality needed to reach the acceptable level of performance, which is 70 points (Annex 2, sections 2 and 4, to be verified).
- A bid is acceptable on quality when its final quality score is 70 points or more (section 7, to be verified). The documents may also require at least 70 points on some criteria (section 5, to be verified).
- The parameter K is between 15 % and 30 %, or up to 40 % with a competitive dialogue (section 9, to be verified).
- For a contract that includes goods, article 15 (to be verified) lets the body add additional costs to the bid prices. Additional costs are costs not included in the bid price that the body would bear over the useful life of the goods. Only the worksheet rows that the bidder marks "no" under "included in the bid price" count in that adjustment.
- Ask your counsel whether software licenses and hosted services count as goods for this purpose.

**Québec municipal bodies (LCOM).** The guide on the act of the Québec ministry of municipal affairs (2nd edition, 2026) describes two systems that weigh quality and price. Each article number below is to be verified.

- Global evaluation of criteria (guide §9.3.2; articles 42 and 54 to 56): price is one of the criteria, and the guide sets no minimum or maximum number of criteria or weights.
- Deferred knowledge of price (guide §9.4.1; articles 43 and 66 to 72): at least 4 criteria, at most 30 points for any criterion out of 100, price in a separate document, a price opened only for an interim score of 70 points or more, and a factor from 0 to 50 that the body records before publication and keeps confidential until the opening.
- The same guide says that no regulation yet sets how a municipal body adjusts prices for additional costs. Use the worksheet only to assess your need and set your budget.

**Sources.** The pass or fail baseline and the use of fixed scales follow the procurement chapter of the Interoperable Europe guidelines. The default weights are FPSOT's own. The World Bank 2026 guidance inspired the attention to handover and total cost, and no text is adapted from it.

**Mandatory reference.** Only the normative FPSOT specification that your tender cites is the mandatory reference.
Cite that specification as the requirement, never this module or an FPSOT guide.

**Not legal advice.** This module is a draft and is not legal advice.

## Clause text

### Variant for Québec public bodies (LCOP and RCTI)

1. **Conditions of conformity.** Each conformance class that module M01 requires, and each mandatory term of these tender documents, is a condition of conformity. A bid that does not meet one of them is rejected. These conditions earn no points.
2. **Quality.** The selection committee evaluates the quality of each conforming bid against the criteria of the grid below. It scores each criterion from 0 to 100 points. The weights total 100 %.
3. **Acceptable level.** For each criterion, the acceptable level of performance is 70 points. It requires these elements: [elements required for the acceptable level, per criterion].
4. **Scale.** For each criterion, the committee gives the score of the anchor that the bid meets:
   - 0 points, no information;
   - 30 points, far below the acceptable level;
   - 50 points, below it;
   - 70 points, meets it;
   - 85 points, goes beyond it on some points;
   - 100 points, goes beyond it on most points.
5. **Acceptable bid.** A bid is acceptable on quality when its final quality score is 70 points or more. [Option: A bid that scores less than 70 points on any of these criteria is rejected: [criteria with a minimum].]
6. **Price.** The organization adjusts the price of each acceptable bid as the RCTI sets, with a parameter K of [K] %.
7. **Additional costs.** [Option, for a contract that includes goods: To set the total cost of acquisition, the organization adds to each bid price the additional costs of the worksheet below. Only the elements that the bidder marks as not included in its bid price count.]
8. **Paid editions.** Each bidder must list each function of its solution that needs a paid edition, a paid module or a paid service. The organization counts its cost in the worksheet.
9. **No tie to FPSOT.** No criterion gives points for membership of FPSOT, for a sponsorship, or for a listing in its register of results.

### Variant for Québec municipal bodies (LCOM), global evaluation of criteria

1. **Conditions of conformity.** Each conformance class that module M01 requires, and each mandatory term of the documents of this open procedure, is a condition of conformity. A bid that does not meet one of them is rejected. These conditions earn no points.
2. **Criteria.** The selection committee evaluates each conforming bid against the criteria of the grid below and against price. The quality criteria share [quality points] points, in the proportions of their weights. Price is worth [price points] points. The total is 100 points.
3. **Scale.** For each quality criterion, the committee gives a share of its points:
   - 0 %, no information;
   - 30 %, far below expectations;
   - 50 %, below expectations;
   - 70 %, meets expectations;
   - 85 %, goes beyond them on some points;
   - 100 %, goes beyond them on most points.
4. **Price.** Each bid receives [price points] points × lowest conforming price ÷ its price.
5. **Award.** The municipal body awards the contract to the conforming bid with the highest total score.
6. **Paid editions.** Each bidder must list each function of its solution that needs a paid edition, a paid module or a paid service, with its price. The cost-element worksheet below does not adjust the bid prices.
7. **No tie to FPSOT.** No criterion gives points for membership of FPSOT, for a sponsorship, or for a listing in its register of results.

### Variant for Québec municipal bodies (LCOM), deferred knowledge of price

1. **Conditions of conformity.** Each conformance class that module M01 requires, and each mandatory term of the documents of this open procedure, is a condition of conformity. A bid that does not meet one of them is rejected. These conditions earn no points.
2. **Criteria.** The selection committee evaluates each conforming bid against the criteria of the grid below, which number at least 4. The criteria total 100 points, and no criterion is worth more than 30 points.
3. **Scale.** For each criterion, the committee gives a share of its points:
   - 0 %, no information;
   - 30 %, far below expectations;
   - 50 %, below expectations;
   - 70 %, meets expectations;
   - 85 %, goes beyond them on some points;
   - 100 %, goes beyond them on most points.
4. **Minimums.** [Option: A bid that does not reach [minimum score] points on [criteria with a minimum] is rejected.]
5. **Price.** The bidder gives its price in a document separate from the rest of its bid. The municipal body opens the price only of a bid whose interim score is 70 points or more and that meets each minimum.
6. **Final score.** The final score is (interim score + factor) × 10,000 ÷ price. The factor is a number from 0 to 50 that the municipal body recorded before it published these documents. The municipal body awards the contract to the conforming bid with the highest final score.
7. **Paid editions.** In its price document, each bidder must list each function of its solution that needs a paid edition, module or service. It gives the price of each. The cost-element worksheet below does not adjust the bid prices.
8. **No tie to FPSOT.** No criterion gives points for membership of FPSOT, for a sponsorship, or for a listing in its register of results.

### Variant for the federal government

1. **Mandatory criteria.** Each conformance class that module M01 requires, and each mandatory term of the solicitation, is a mandatory criterion. A bid that does not meet one of them is non-responsive. Mandatory criteria earn no points.
2. **Point-rated criteria.** Canada scores each responsive bid against the criteria of the grid below, each from 0 to 5, on the anchors below. The weights total 100.
3. **Scale.** The anchors are:
   - 0, no information;
   - 1, far below expectations;
   - 2, below expectations;
   - 3, meets expectations;
   - 4, goes beyond them on some points;
   - 5, goes beyond them on most points.
4. **Price.** Canada evaluates the total cost of the bid over [life of the solution], including the cost elements of the worksheet below that the Bidder prices.
5. **Paid editions.** Each Bidder must list each function of its solution that needs a paid edition, a paid module or a paid service, with its price.
6. **No tie to FPSOT.** No criterion gives points for membership of FPSOT, for a sponsorship, or for a listing in its register of results.

## Cost-element worksheet

The buyer fills in this worksheet before it publishes the tender, and puts it in the tender documents.
Each bidder gives the values of its bid for each element, and says whether its bid price already includes it.
L is the life of the solution in years, which the buyer sets in the field [life of the solution].

| Element | What it covers | Formula | Included in the bid price? (yes/no) | Bidder's value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 Licenses | Licenses, subscriptions and paid editions over the life | yearly cost × L, with the yearly increase that the bid states | [yes/no] | [value] |
| E2 Changes | Changes that the buyer expects, such as new versions of the cited specification | days of work expected × daily rate | [yes/no] | [value] |
| E3 Interoperability | Interfaces with the systems that the buyer names | number of interfaces × cost per interface in the bid | [yes/no] | [value] |
| E4 Training | Training of the staff of the buyer | number of people × cost per person | [yes/no] | [value] |
| E5 Support | Support and maintenance over the life | yearly cost × L | [yes/no] | [value] |
| E6 Data migration at exit | Export, transformation and reload into another solution | volume of data × cost per unit, plus fixed costs | [yes/no] | [value] |
| E7 Other | Any other element that the buyer names and can measure | as the buyer sets | [yes/no] | [value] |
| Total of additional costs | | Sum of the values of the rows marked "no" | | [total] |

## Fields

| Field | Meaning | Suggested value |
|---|---|---|
| [elements required for the acceptable level, per criterion] | RCTI: for each criterion, the elements of quality a bid must show to score 70 points. | None |
| [criteria with a minimum] | The criteria on which a bid must reach a minimum score, or remove the option. | Remove the option |
| [minimum score] | LCOM, deferred price: the minimum score on those criteria. | 70 % of the points of the criterion |
| [K] | The RCTI parameter K, between 15 % and 30 %, or up to 40 % with a competitive dialogue. | 20 % |
| [quality points] | LCOM, global evaluation: the points shared by the quality criteria. | 70 |
| [price points] | LCOM, global evaluation: the points for price. 100 minus [quality points]. | 30 |
| [life of the solution] | The number of years over which the buyer counts costs. | 5 years |
| [weight taken from the others] | The weight of the optional accessibility criterion, taken from the other weights. | Remove the row, or 5 |
| [yes/no] | Filled in by each bidder. | None |
| [value] | Filled in by each bidder. | None |
| [total] | Filled in by each bidder. | None |
