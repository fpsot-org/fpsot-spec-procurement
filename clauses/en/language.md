## Drafting note

Remove this note before you publish the tender.

**Purpose.** This clause requires the solution and its services in French, and, for federal buyers, in both official languages.

**Functional need.** Staff and the public must be able to use the solution in their language, and the law of each regime sets duties on language.

**Québec.** The Charter of the French language requires French for the contracts of the Québec administration and their related writings.
Every tender document and contract must also include one or more language clauses.
The Québec ministry of the French language publishes model clauses for this in its guide *Contrats*.
Insert the model clauses that fit your contract, in their current version, where this module says so.
This module does not copy them, because the ministry updates them.

**Federal.** Federal bodies have duties under the Official Languages Act. Ask your counsel which of them apply to the contract.

**Mandatory reference.** Only the normative FPSOT specification that your tender cites is the mandatory reference.
Cite that specification as the requirement, never this module or an FPSOT guide.

**Not legal advice.** This module is a draft and is not legal advice.

## Clause text

### Variant for Québec public bodies (LCOP and RCTI)

1. [Insert here the model language clauses of the ministry of the French language that apply to this contract.]
2. The user interface, the messages, the online help and the documentation of the solution must be in French. They may also be in other languages, provided that the French version is at least as complete and as easy to reach.
3. The supplier must give training and support in French.
4. Where the solution includes a product of FPSOT, rules LOC-003 and LOC-030 of the FPSOT internationalization specification, version [x.y], or equivalent, apply.

### Variant for Québec municipal bodies (LCOM)

1. [Insert here the model language clauses that apply to this contract, from the guide of the ministry of the French language.]
2. The user interface, the messages, the online help and the documentation of the solution must be in French. They may also be in other languages, provided that the French version is at least as complete and as easy to reach.
3. The supplier must give training and support in French.
4. Where the solution includes a product of FPSOT, rules LOC-003 and LOC-030 of the FPSOT internationalization specification, version [x.y], or equivalent, apply.

### Variant for the federal government

1. The user interface, the messages, the online help and the documentation of the Work must be in English and in French. Both must be of equal quality and available at the same time.
2. The Contractor must give training and support in both official languages, at the choice of each user.
3. Where the Work includes a product of FPSOT, rules LOC-003 and LOC-030 of the FPSOT internationalization specification, version [x.y], or equivalent, apply.
