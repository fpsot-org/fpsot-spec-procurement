# Scope

This Working Group develops the FPSOT procurement specification.

The specification covers the procurement material that FPSOT publishes for public bodies: clause packs and clause modules that buyers adapt for their own tenders, their versions, languages and license, their variants by legal regime, the rule for citing a versioned FPSOT specification and its conformance classes with "or equivalent" and with conformance evidence, the content of modules on licenses and intellectual property, repositories, data and exit, accessibility, language, award and cost, and security, the conditions under which a specification is ready for tenders, a public register of conformance results, and the neutrality of FPSOT towards tenders. It includes draft clause modules in English and French.

It does not cover full tender documents, the administrative clauses of governments, the procurement decisions of any buyer, the assessment of any bid, or legal advice.

Any changes of Scope are not retroactive.
